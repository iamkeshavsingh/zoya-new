Our Creations

DANCE OF THE PEACOCK

The tiara designed for the beauty pageant draws inspiration from the majestic peacock, a symbol of grace, strength, and captivating beauty. The peacock, with its vibrant plumage, mirrors the elegance, poise, and power of a woman-radiant and enigmatic, bold yet refined. She embraces her allure with confidence, folly aware of her being
This tiara features shades of green tourmaline and blue topaz, with hints of pink tourmaline and garnet. The genistones echo the iridescent hues of the peacock's tail. The seamless combination makes the tiara a modern masterpiece of luxury and sophistication, capturing the juxtaposition of nature and high couture.
